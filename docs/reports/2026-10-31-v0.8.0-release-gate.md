## Gate observations

- **Headless correctness.** The physical-GPU suite reports no failed or
  skipped renderer assertions, now with the transmission check and the ten
  material fixtures' references under both integrators and both light-sampling
  modes.
- **Hydra and the installed host.** The material-fixture checks passed on CPU
  and GPU for all ten fixtures, and the skinned-mesh checks through both of
  UsdImaging's front ends. This is the first run of the full suite with every
  v0.8.0 task together: the task reports each ran selected tests, not this
  suite. The installed usdview check, which asserts the fallback environment on
  a stage with no lights, passed in 26.59 seconds.
- **Clean validation and synchronization.** Synchronization validation is
  enabled in the suite and in both viewport smoke runs, with no validation
  message in any of them.
- **Interactive viewport smoke test.** A visible 1280x720 window with
  validation, FIFO presentation and the denoiser on presented 240 frames of
  the Cornell box with the variance view split against the filtered image,
  and 240 frames of `usdview-smoke.usda` through the Hydra-enabled build
  (launched with its `run-lotus-viewport.cmd`) with the history-length view
  split; no present skips, swapchain recreations, validation messages or
  readbacks. Both passed at their first attempt.
- **Research tier.** The SVGF comparison under both integrators, the ReSTIR
  control procedures and two ReSTIR campaign variants that share the fixed
  check passed.

## Findings

- `lotus-viewport-present` lost the device at frame 2 at its first run in both
  viewport trees and once more on a rerun in the Hydra-enabled tree, and the
  core viewport launch lost it once (`vkWaitForFences` returning
  `VK_ERROR_DEVICE_LOST`). That is four losses in the gate's 14 presenting
  launches, all with the signature of the
  [known driver fault](../reference/SUPPORTED_CONFIGURATIONS.md#driver-limitations).
  The nvlddmkm event log was not read for these runs. Each test passed on a
  rerun.
- The Hydra build left 42 of its 64 objects without dependency records. Each had
  just been compiled, so none is stale in this gate, but an incremental build
  after a further header change would not rebuild them.

Not rerun: the research tier's fourteen other ReSTIR campaign variants, whose
shared check is exercised by the two above; the survey's third-party stages,
which the task reports rendered and which are not redistributable; and the
hosted runs, which the tag starts.

## Repeatable package

Two `ost package --profile lookdev --intent hydra --json` invocations
returned the same archive digest:

```text
sha256:06b61380747130140a882fcf317e1bc706fd76aa23de30d3ef31809645935560
```

The 9,519,565-byte archive has 59 manifest entries; since v0.7.0 it adds
the five new fixtures' references and the fallback image with its licence and
notice. The `scripts/release.py stage` command verified package/version/target
identity, digest agreement, the actual archive hash, SBOM identity and required
products, including those additions. Staged assets are under
`build/release-v0.8.0-bundle`; tag-driven Actions rebuild the published package
on the hosted runner.

## Scope disposition

The [release record](../releases/v0.8.0.md) defines the shipped scope: every
task of the
[v0.8.0 milestone](../roadmap/README.md#v080--materials--lighting), each closed
by its own report's decision, and the fallback environment image, which was
requested outside the survey. Renderer Phases 8 and 9 remain open. The viewport
is not in the Hydra package; its intermittent second-frame device loss remains a
[driver limitation](../reference/SUPPORTED_CONFIGURATIONS.md#driver-limitations).
