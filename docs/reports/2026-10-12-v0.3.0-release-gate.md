## Required gate observations

- Synchronization validation was enabled on both physical-GPU
  configurations with no validation messages.
- The fixed Cornell scene at 1, 16, 64, 256 and 1024 spp passed the
  committed mean/variance tolerance under BSDF sampling and under light
  sampling, through both integrators. The committed sample sequence
  reproduced the reference bit for bit; a 10% brighter red wall was
  rejected (maximum absolute z 6.32 under BSDF sampling, 5.88 under light
  sampling). References were not regenerated.
- `renderer.path.analytic_lights` and `renderer.path.environment` passed
  their independent oracles under both estimators and integrators; the
  Stinson Beach floor's per-sample variance was 4,063 times lower under
  light sampling.
- Hydra discovery, delegate creation, CPU buffers, deterministic sample
  settings, integrator and light-sampling selection, analytic lights,
  textured domes, materials/textures/normals/opacity, instancing and
  render-pass selection passed. The usdview smoke test, including both
  menu toggles, passed.
- The standalone viewport's disposition remains the previously measured
  [foundation review](2026-10-10-foundation-closure.md); this session did
  not rerun the viewport.

## Package

Two `ost package --profile lookdev --intent hydra --json` invocations of the
validated Hydra build returned the same archive digest:

```text
sha256:c0029765914e2ad0bb99b439f1bad54cb0993d4edc9c528c6366bb34a99616cf
```

The 2,528,658-byte archive has 33 manifest entries, including the
validation assets (`StinsonBeach.hdr`, its licence and manifest) and
`wavefront.comp.spv` beside the headless runner and the plugin.
`scripts/release.py stage` and `bundle` checked the package identity,
archive hash and required products, and assembled the source archive,
`SHA256SUMS` and notes under `build/release-v0.3.0-bundle`. The published
assets are rebuilt by the tag-driven workflow on the hosted runner; this
local bundle verifies the same helper path.
