## Gate observations

- **Headless correctness.** The physical-GPU suite reports no failed or
  skipped renderer assertions: deterministic reference images (1,000
  bootstrap frames, the Cornell box against its committed tolerances),
  NEE/MIS and environment sampling, CPU/GPU reservoir arithmetic and every
  headless comparison workload under both integrators. Committed references
  are unchanged since v0.4.0.
- **Clean validation and synchronization.** Synchronization validation is
  enabled in the suite and in both viewport smoke runs, with no validation
  message in any of them.
- **Installed Hydra regression.** The installed usdview check passed in
  106.50 seconds; the Hydra temporal contract and GPU tests passed with the
  filter setting.
- **Renderer Phase 5 closure.** The [exit review](2026-10-17-renderer-phase5-exit.md)
  shipped in [v0.5.1](../releases/v0.5.1.md) is unchanged; the default suite's
  reservoir arithmetic, GPU lifecycle, distant benchmark under both
  integrators and Hydra reservoir checks pass, as do the research tier's
  ReSTIR control procedures.
- **Deterministic denoiser validation.** `lotus-renderer-temporal-gpu`
  compares moments, variance and 38 filtered frames per integrator with the
  CPU oracle at every pixel. The research tier's SVGF comparison, at 32x32,
  rendered each workload's first sequence again from a new cut and
  reproduced every raw, temporal and filtered value bit for bit under both
  integrators, and the two integrators' errors were identical for both the
  static and the moving room.
- **Interactive viewport smoke test.** A visible 1280x720 window with
  validation, FIFO presentation and the denoiser on presented 240 frames of
  the Cornell box with the variance view split against the filtered image,
  and 240 frames of `usdview-smoke.usda` through the Hydra-enabled build
  with the history-length view split; no present skips, swapchain
  recreations, validation messages or readbacks. The viewports' scripted
  interaction tests step every control and view.

validation layer disabled through the loader, reference integrator unless

Milliseconds; the wavefront path trace is the sum of its kernels. They

48 per pixel. These are observations at unlocked clocks, not a new
baseline.

## Findings fixed on the way

- The research tier's `lotus-renderer-svgf-benchmark-*` still expected the
  filter to hold 32 bytes per pixel, which the compact guides of
  LOTUS-SVGF-03 made 48; that change did not rerun the research tier. The
  check now expects 48 bytes per pixel, and both tests pass.
- `lotus-viewport-hydra-interaction` lost the device at its second frame
  once (`vkWaitForFences` returning `VK_ERROR_DEVICE_LOST`), the
  [known driver fault](2026-10-18-viewport-device-loss.md), and passed in
  three reruns.

Not rerun: the research tier's sixteen ReSTIR campaign variants, whose code
and reservoir checks are unchanged, and the hosted runs, which the tag
starts.

## Repeatable package

Two `ost package --profile lookdev --intent hydra --json` invocations
returned the same archive digest:

```text
sha256:8fc6737604495468d9bc9e786bc90f98fe1529ef56960c7359bb5b085d47423e
```

The 3,060,226-byte archive has 36 manifest entries. The
`scripts/release.py stage` command verified package/version/target identity,
digest agreement, the actual archive hash, SBOM identity and required
products, including shaders beside both the headless runner and Hydra
plugin, reference images and licensed validation assets. Staged assets are
under `build/release-v0.6.0-bundle`; tag-driven Actions rebuild the published
package on the hosted runner.
