## Gate observations

- **Headless correctness.** The physical-GPU suite reports no failed or
  skipped renderer assertions: deterministic reference images, NEE/MIS and
  environment sampling, the five material fixtures against their 4096-spp
  references under both integrators and both light-sampling modes, CPU/GPU
  reservoir and temporal oracles, and every headless comparison workload
  under both integrators. The light crossing hierarchy is the default in
  all of them.
- **Hydra and the installed host.** Both material-fixture checks passed:
  UsdImaging's snapshot equals each fixture's mirror, and each Hydra image
  equals the backend's image of the mirror bit for bit. The installed
  usdview check passed in 24.88 seconds. The Hydra tree had not been
  rebuilt since several of the practical-foundation changes; it now runs
  them with the same backend.
- **Memory changes under every frontend.** The residency benchmark checks
  in the default suite pass with and without readback; the headless and
  viewport runs below read back nothing.
- **Clean validation and synchronization.** Synchronization validation is
  enabled in the suite and in both viewport smoke runs, with no validation
  message in any of them.
- **Interactive viewport smoke test.** A visible 1280x720 window with
  validation, FIFO presentation and the denoiser on presented 240 frames of
  the Cornell box with the variance view split against the filtered image,
  and 240 frames of `usdview-smoke.usda` through the Hydra-enabled build
  (launched with its `run-lotus-viewport.cmd`) with the history-length view
  split; no present skips, swapchain recreations, validation messages or
  readbacks.
- **Research tier.** The SVGF comparison under both integrators, the ReSTIR
  control procedures and two ReSTIR campaign variants that share the fixed
  check passed.

The filter holds 44,236,800 bytes, 48 per pixel, as in
[v0.6.0](2026-10-18-v0.6.0-release-gate.md#gpu-timings). The Hydra run's

[closure](2026-10-28-practical-foundation-closure.md) owns the measured
effects of this release's changes.

## Findings fixed on the way

- `lotus-renderer-restir-benchmark-reference` and `-wavefront`, and the
  research tier's campaign variants sharing their check, still expected the
  reservoirs to hold 864 bytes per pixel with temporal reuse and 1,280 with
  spatial reuse when frames read back. The
  [reservoir history split](2026-10-26-reservoir-history-split.md) made
  them 608 and 768, as the
  [reservoir reference](../reference/RESTIR.md) states; that change ran
  `lotus-renderer-restir-gpu` and the residency checks but not this
  benchmark, and hosted CI skips it for lack of a GPU. The check now
  expects 608 and 768 bytes per pixel; images and every other field were
  unchanged.
- `lotus-viewport-interaction` lost the device at its second frame in two
  of seven runs and `lotus-viewport-hydra-interaction` in two of four
  (`vkWaitForFences` returning `VK_ERROR_DEVICE_LOST`), the
  [known driver fault](../reference/SUPPORTED_CONFIGURATIONS.md#driver-limitations);
  each passed on a rerun.

Not rerun: the research tier's fourteen other ReSTIR campaign variants,
whose code is unchanged and whose shared check is exercised by the two
above, and the hosted runs, which the tag starts.

## Repeatable package

Two `ost package --profile lookdev --intent hydra --json` invocations
returned the same archive digest:

```text
sha256:2e523623c801441ca20cd8aaf357647dd2d7192aa15d87f253de8dd9f50c7395
```

The 5,641,429-byte archive has 46 manifest entries; since v0.6.0 it adds
the five material fixtures' references, 2,850,956 bytes uncompressed. The
`scripts/release.py stage` command verified package/version/target identity,
digest agreement, the actual archive hash, SBOM identity and required
products, including shaders beside both the headless runner and Hydra
plugin, every reference image and licensed validation assets. Staged assets
are under `build/release-v0.7.0-bundle`; tag-driven Actions rebuild the
published package on the hosted runner.
