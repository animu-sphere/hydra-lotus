## Gate observations

- **Headless correctness.** The physical-GPU suite reports no failed or
  skipped renderer assertions. The Reference preset is the default, and the
  committed references, unchanged since v0.8.0, still pass under both
  integrators and both light-sampling modes.
- **Hydra and the installed host.** The Hydra temporal checks, which cover
  the presets, the invalidation table's camera cut and Balanced's
  convergence, passed on the GPU. This is the first run of the full suite
  with every v0.9.0 task together: the task reports each ran selected tests,
  not this suite. The installed usdview check passed in 31.53 seconds.
- **Clean validation and synchronization.** Synchronization validation is
  enabled in the suite and in both viewport smoke runs, with no validation
  message in any of them.
- **Interactive viewport smoke test.** A visible 1280x720 window with
  validation and FIFO presentation presented 240 frames of the Cornell box
  under `--preset interactive`, with the variance view split against the
  filtered image, and 240 frames of `usdview-smoke.usda` through the
  Hydra-enabled build (launched with its `run-lotus-viewport.cmd`) under
  `--preset balanced` with the history-length view split. The still Hydra
  view settled: its last frame holds 239 samples per pixel. No present
  skips, swapchain recreations, validation messages or readbacks. Both
  passed at their first attempt.
- **Research tier.** The SVGF comparison under both integrators, which
  includes the contract's five workloads, the ReSTIR control procedures and
  two ReSTIR campaign variants that share the fixed check passed.

## GPU timings

From the two smoke runs above, with validation and synchronization
validation active, FIFO presentation and unlocked clocks; reference
integrator, five filter iterations. Means per frame over the frames that ran
each pass, in milliseconds.

| Scene | Preset | Path trace | Surfaces | Accumulation | Variance | Filter | GPU frame |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cornell box | Interactive | 2.26 | 0.93 | 0.24 | 0.57 | 2.56 | 6.56 |
| `usdview-smoke.usda` (Hydra) | Balanced | 0.43 | 1.28 | 0.03 | 0.03 | 11.37 | 1.81 |

The Cornell run's first frame took 135 ms and its median frame is 5.70 ms.
The Hydra run's first eight frames, before the view settled, took 6–34 ms
at rising clocks and are the only frames that ran the filter, which explains
its mean; its 232 settled frames took 1.33 ms on average (median 1.54 ms),
with no filter. The filter holds 44,236,800 bytes, 48 per pixel, as in
[v0.8.0](2026-10-31-v0.8.0-release-gate.md#gpu-timings). These are
observations, not a new baseline: unlocked clocks do not support comparing
them with v0.8.0's.

## Findings

- `lotus-viewport-hydra-camera` lost the device at frame 2 at its first run
  in the Hydra-enabled tree (`vkWaitForFences` returning
  `VK_ERROR_DEVICE_LOST`) and passed on a rerun. That is one loss in the
  gate's 14 presenting launches, with the signature of the
  [known driver fault](../reference/SUPPORTED_CONFIGURATIONS.md#driver-limitations).
  The nvlddmkm event log was not read for this run.
- The Hydra build left 45 of its 64 objects without dependency records. They
  were deleted after the build, so the next build recompiles them; none was
  stale in this gate.
- The local OpenStrata CLI, 0.23.19, is newer than CI's pinned 0.23.14. The
  hosted runs, which the tag starts, use the pinned version.

Not rerun: the research tier's fourteen other ReSTIR campaign variants, whose
shared check is exercised by the two above; the milestone's ghosting
campaign, whose candidates no preset selects; and the hosted runs, which the
tag starts.

## Repeatable package

Two `ost package --profile lookdev --intent hydra --json` invocations
returned the same archive digest:

```text
sha256:ad9462a26b0f0fd856541ea3707d91399ac9e0e51e33737783a225f3408a45a6
```

The 9,540,697-byte archive has 59 manifest entries, as many as v0.8.0's. The
`scripts/release.py stage` command verified package/version/target identity,
digest agreement, the actual archive hash, SBOM identity and required
products. Staged assets are under `build/release-v0.9.0-bundle`; tag-driven
Actions rebuild the published package on the hosted runner.
