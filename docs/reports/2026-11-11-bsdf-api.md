# BSDF API

## Evaluation brief

LOTUS-MATERIALX-02 implements the accepted [BSDF API](../design/BSDF_API.md)
with one closure, `UsdPreviewSurface`'s. Adopt it if `bsdf.slang` provides
`BsdfEvaluate`, `BsdfPdf` and `BsdfSample` with the contract's measures,
both integrators call only them, `lotus-bsdf-test` passes the six
assertions in the `fast` tier, and every committed reference image is
unchanged ([acceptance](../design/BSDF_API.md#9-acceptance-for-lotus-materialx-02)).

## What changed

- **Closure.** `PreviewClosure` (the former `SurfaceBsdf`, built by
  `MakePreviewClosure`) is wrapped in `BsdfClosure`, a model tag and the
  preview data; `SurfaceHit.bsdf` is a `BsdfClosure`. The three functions
  dispatch on the tag with a `switch`. The old names are gone.
- **Measures.** `BsdfEvaluate` and `BsdfPdf` cover the whole sphere: below
  the shading normal they return the rough interface's transmission, a
  solid's refraction (Walter et al., radiance mode, `1/η²`) and a thin
  wall's mirrored reflection, each times the transmitted share and its
  Fresnel split. The reflection side's arithmetic is unchanged. A rough
  transmission sample now reports `pdf = BsdfPdf(wo, wi)`; nothing reads it,
  since its light is unweighted.
- **Weights.** The closure documents them: the mixture ratio without
  transmission (D cancelled analytically for a pure metal), component
  ratios with it.
- **Integrators.** `transport.slang`'s next event estimation and
  `restir.slang`'s targets and MIS weight ask for the reflection side
  explicitly (`wi.z > 0`), which returns the previous values exactly.
  `RestirSurface` packing moved into `StoreClosure` and `LoadClosure`,
  with the same 128-byte layout
  ([IR v2 §6.3](../design/MATERIAL_IR_V2.md#63-what-stays-for-lotus-materialx-02)).
- **Names.** The sample record is `BsdfSampleResult`: Slang rejects a type
  and a function both named `BsdfSample`. The contract records this and
  that the layered lobes, alpha-clamped, are never delta.

## The test

`lotus-bsdf-test` (ctest `lotus-renderer-bsdf`, `fast` tier, Vulkan-enabled
builds) compiles `validation/bsdf-probe.slang`, which imports the real
`bsdf.slang`, to C++ with `slangc -target cpp`; the prelude is embedded in
the output, so the build needs no SDK header path. The probe exports the
three functions over float arrays.

**Grid.** Section 7.2's axes without points that differ only in an input
the closure ignores (metallic in the specular workflow, a solid's side or
a thin wall without transmission, the index of a metal): 85 closures per
roughness and `wo`, 2,040 configurations, 1,360 of them from roughness
0.1 up. 50,000 samples each, seeded by the configuration's index;
`lotus-bsdf-test --only <index>` reruns one with its bins printed.

**Bins.** 16 × 32 equal-area bins with `y`, the normal to the plane of
incidence, as the polar axis: every lobe peak then lies on the equator,
never at a pole, and `z = 0` is a bin edge. Each bin's `BsdfEvaluate` and
`BsdfPdf` integrals use adaptive 4 × 4 Gauss–Legendre cells, split at
least once, refined toward known lobe centres to an eighth of alpha, and
then until a cell agrees with its children to 1e-5 per bin plus 3e-4
relative, at most eight levels. Both tolerances are below the standard
error of a bin expecting five samples.

**Assertions**, as section 7.3 numbers them:

1. Every non-delta sample's `pdf` equals `BsdfPdf` at its direction to
   1e-5 relative and is positive; `transmitted` matches `wi.z < 0`; delta
   samples have `pdf` 0; weights are finite and non-negative; no
   quadrature node has a positive `BsdfEvaluate` where `BsdfPdf` is 0. The
   weight equals `BsdfEvaluate / BsdfPdf` to 1e-3 where the closure
   documents the mixture ratio: no transmission, or a rough interface
   alone (one lobe per side).
2. Per bin and channel, the mean weight equals the bin's `BsdfEvaluate`
   integral within 6 standard errors plus 1e-5 and 1e-3 relative. Bins
   expecting fewer than 5 samples are pooled; a cell's standard error is
   at least the one its integrals predict for a constant weight, since a
   pooled cell may hold no samples.
3. `∫ BsdfPdf` equals the non-delta success rate within 5 binomial
   standard errors plus 5e-4, and is at most 1 + 5e-4.
4. χ² of the non-delta samples over the bins, expected counts scaled to
   their observed total, low-expectation bins pooled, at 0.01 divided by
   the 1,360 configurations.
5. With a smooth interface (roughness 0 or index 1): the delta fraction is
   the transmitted share, delta directions are the mirror or the Snell
   refraction (straight through for a wall or index 1), weights are 1 and
   `tint/η²` (`tint` for a wall), and the reflected fraction is the exact
   Fresnel reflectance (`2R/(1+R)` for a wall).
6. A rough interface alone (roughness 0.1 up): each transmission sample's
   `BsdfEvaluate` and `BsdfPdf` match Walter et al.'s equations, written
   independently in the test, to 1%.

### Thresholds fixed in development

- **The quadrature split.** Unsplit, a rough solid's refraction bin
  (roughness 1, index 1.5 entering, `cos θo` 0.3) was accepted when its
  rule and its four children's agreed by coincidence, 2.6% above the
  value a 4,000² midpoint sum of the same probe gives; an independent
  double-precision Python integration of the density matched the
  midpoint sum and the sampler's success rate (0.9687 against 0.9693).
  Hence the minimum split: the bin's 2 × 2 cells and their children
  already disagree there.
- **The integral's precision.** At roughness 0.1 (alpha 0.01), float
  rounding in GGX's `h.z² (α² − 1) + 1` near the peak moves `∫ BsdfPdf` by
  about 1e-4 (a float and a double evaluation of a pure metal's lobe
  differ by 9e-5); four interface configurations at that roughness
  differed from their success rate by 2.6e-4 to 3.4e-4. Assertion 3
  allows 5e-4, and assertion 4 scales its expected counts to the observed
  total instead of including the delta-or-failed outcome, whose small
  expected count turned that rounding into χ² 81 on 32 degrees of freedom
  for a pure metal at normal incidence.

### Sensitivity

Each of three deliberate defects in `bsdf.slang` fails the test, with the
final settings above (configurations failing, of 2,040; a configuration
can fail several assertions):

| Defect | Failing | By assertion |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Refraction value without `1/η²` | 514 | 1: 381, 2: 1,188, 6: 381 |
| Refraction Jacobian without the `/η` | 576 | 2: 1,064, 3: 388, 4: 4, 6: 758 |
| A thin wall's transmission density with `R` for `1 − R` | 288 | 2: 495, 3: 192, 4: 43, 6: 384 |

The counts per assertion count each configuration's first four failures.

## Verification

On an NVIDIA RTX A5000 (Windows, MSVC 19.51, Release, Vulkan SDK
1.4.350):

- **The test.** Passed in 5 s on 32 threads and in 22 s pinned to four
  cores, a hosted runner's count (87 s before the tolerances and sample
  count were set to the values above). Largest observed values: relative
  `pdf` error 0, mixture-ratio error 5.5e-7, estimator 4.9 standard
  errors, normalization difference 0.0076 (within its 5 standard
  errors), smallest χ² p 6.4e-4 against the threshold 7.4e-6, and
  transmission error 0.54% against Walter et al.
- **References.** `lotus-headless --write-reference` and
  `--write-material-reference` regenerated the Cornell reference and the
  ten material fixtures, mean and variance: all 22 files are
  byte-identical to the committed ones. No rounding difference arose.
- **Core tree**, run at targeted spots rather than as the whole suite:
  `lotus-renderer-evidence` (both integrators against the committed
  references), `lotus-renderer-restir-gpu` (the stored closure),
  `lotus-renderer-temporal-gpu`, `lotus-renderer-bsdf` and
  `lotus-test-tiers` passed.
- **Hydra tree** (`--profile lookdev --intent hydra`):
  `lotus-renderer-hydra-integrator`, `-hydra-restir-gpu`,
  `-hydra-material`, `-hydra-material-gpu`, `-hydra-material-fixtures`,
  `-hydra-material-fixtures-gpu` (both integrators per fixture),
  `lotus-renderer-bsdf` and `lotus-test-tiers` passed. The rest of the
  Hydra and core suites was not rerun.

## Decision

Adopt. The acceptance's five conditions hold: `bsdf.slang` provides the
three functions with the contract's measures and documents its weights;
`transport.slang` and `restir.slang` call only them; `lotus-bsdf-test`
passes in the `fast` tier; every committed reference is byte-identical;
and the [transmission contract](../design/TRANSMISSION.md#5-sampling),
the [scene reference](../reference/SCENE.md#path-tracing) and
[IR v2 §6.3](../design/MATERIAL_IR_V2.md#63-what-stays-for-lotus-materialx-02)
point to the BSDF API and record the packing rule. LOTUS-MATERIALX-03
(layer and mix composition) is next.
