# v0.5.0 — Experimental ReSTIR DI release

Released on 2026-10-16 under Apache-2.0. This snapshot adds opt-in
direct-light reservoirs under the reference and wavefront integrators,
headless and through Hydra. Progressive NEE/MIS remains the default.
This is an experimental feature release; ongoing scope and release targets
remain in the [canonical roadmap](../roadmap/README.md#status-at-a-glance).

## Shipped capabilities

- Initial candidate generation and weighted reservoir selection for emissive
  triangles, analytic lights and textured environments, with power, uniform
  and equal-mixture proposals.
- Temporal reuse with shared reprojection, receiver reevaluation, bounded
  represented counts, exact source targets and support-corrected weights.
  Spatial reuse reads immutable input reservoirs, rejects surface boundaries
  and retains resolved weights for subsequent reuse.
- Current-receiver visibility and transmittance, including reuse of a
  same-frame result only for an unchanged light-slot/UV sample. Complementary
  reservoir/BSDF direct-light weights retain BSDF-found emission and improve
  the reproduced narrow-glossy fixture.
- Hydra settings for candidate count, temporal/spatial reuse, history cap
  and neighbour radius, plus six final-reservoir diagnostic AOVs. Disabling
  ReSTIR restores the selected progressive estimator.
- Separate GPU timestamps, reservoir/history memory accounting, independent
  CPU/GPU arithmetic and lighting checks, and headless static/moving
  comparisons across many-light, mixed-boundary and glossy workloads.
  Repeated-report reduction records observed quality budgets, unattained
  thresholds, regional errors and temporal residual correlation.

Detailed changes are in the [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md#050---2026-10-16).
The [reservoir contract](../reference/RESTIR.md) owns settings, estimator
semantics, diagnostics and limits; the
[release gate](../reports/2026-10-16-v0.5.0-release-gate.md) records verification.

## Correctness and measurement

The [CPU arithmetic](../reports/2026-10-13-restir-reservoir.md) and
[differing-target combination](../reports/2026-10-13-restir-reuse-arithmetic.md)
checks use independent discrete integrals, visibility and bounded counts.
[Initial](../reports/2026-10-13-restir-initial.md),
[temporal](../reports/2026-10-13-restir-temporal.md) and
[spatial](../reports/2026-10-14-restir-spatial.md) GPU checks compare arithmetic
with CPU oracles and images with independent lighting integrals and NEE/MIS.
They also exercise resets, failed-frame recovery, overflow and current
visibility. [Complementary BSDF checks](../reports/2026-10-16-restir-bsdf-partition.md)
add independent per-pixel reconstruction and GGX quadrature.

[Moving USD stages and installed usdview](../reports/2026-10-14-restir-hydra.md)
exercise both integrators, all four reservoir modes, controls, diagnostic
AOVs, motion, cuts and deterministic NEE/MIS restoration.
[Repeated budgets](../reports/2026-10-15-restir-budgets.md),
[mixed boundaries](../reports/2026-10-15-restir-boundaries.md),
[temporal residuals](../reports/2026-10-16-restir-correlation.md),
[narrow-glossy comparisons](../reports/2026-10-16-restir-bsdf-partition.md) and
[rough dielectric/sphere-light comparisons](../reports/2026-10-16-restir-glossy-spheres.md)
provide bounded measurements, including adverse results. These establish
neither general unbiasedness nor a repeatable reuse speedup.

## Compatibility and delivery

The `v0.5.0` tag publishes the Windows x86_64 CY2026 lookdev/Hydra package
`lotus-0.5.0-cy2026-windows-x86_64-py313-lookdev--hydra.tar.zst`, its manifest
and SPDX SBOM, `hydra-lotus-0.5.0-src.tar.gz` and `SHA256SUMS` through the
[release workflow](../guides/RELEASING.md). The binary package requires
matching OpenUSD 26.08/Python 3.13 and Vulkan runtimes.

Measured configuration: Windows 11 x86_64, MSVC 14.51, OpenStrata 0.23.14,
NVIDIA RTX A5000 and OpenUSD 26.08/Python 3.13 for Hydra. Other configurations
build from source with the [building guide](../guides/BUILDING.md); measured
GPU requirements remain in the
[configuration reference](../reference/SUPPORTED_CONFIGURATIONS.md#requirements).

Core reservoir modes, proposal/settings and diagnostic records are additive.
Hydra `lotus:restir`, `lotus:restirTemporal` and `lotus:restirSpatial` are
disabled by default. Reuse requires ray queries and colour clears, renders
fresh batches and keeps the Hydra pass unconverged. Initial-only mode retains
deterministic progressive accumulation. Temporal radiance accumulation is
controlled separately.

## Known limitations at release

- Temporal and combined reuse increase static convergence error in the rough
  dielectric/sphere-light fixture. Positive residual correlation is measured;
  the short sequences and finite references do not distinguish bias from
  rare-event variance. Slightly improved moving error does not establish
  improved static convergence or absence of ghosting.
- Candidate generation, metadata, receiver reevaluation, support correction
  and reuse can cost more than NEE/MIS. The measured glossy workloads establish
  no repeatable equal-quality speedup for combined reuse. ReSTIR is an
  opt-in research choice, with NEE/MIS available for comparison and fallback.
- Broader glossy, multibounce, production-scene bias and artifact assessment
  remains open. The latest regional benchmark extensions use bounded
  one-bounce fixtures and finite-spp references; screen bands are not
  material or disocclusion masks. Matched integrators share draws and are
  not independent trials.
- Reuse inherits nearest-pixel reprojection, rigid-motion correspondence,
  explicit cuts/compatibility keys and one retained view per Hydra delegate.
  Deformation, instance reorder and view-dependent changes require care;
  radiance accumulation has its own
  [temporal limitations](../reference/TEMPORAL.md).
- Denoising, GI reservoirs, production material expansion and light
  acceleration remain outside this snapshot. The viewport still presents
  the bootstrap triangle; readback is blocking, Hydra ID channels remain
  clear sentinels, and Linux, macOS and AMD/Intel GPUs are unmeasured.

Remaining investigations stay in the [active roadmap](../roadmap/current.md),
with precise behavior in the [reservoir](../reference/RESTIR.md),
[scene/material](../reference/SCENE.md), [AOV](../reference/AOVS.md) and
[configuration](../reference/SUPPORTED_CONFIGURATIONS.md) references.
