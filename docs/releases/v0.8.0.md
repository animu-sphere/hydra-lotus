# v0.8.0 — Materials and lighting release

Released on 2026-10-31 under Apache-2.0. This release completes the
[v0.8.0 Materials & Lighting milestone](../roadmap/README.md#v080--materials--lighting):
Lotus renders what 18 third-party USD stages showed it was missing, each gap
chosen by an [asset gap survey](../reports/2026-10-29-asset-gap-survey.md#decision).
It ships selected subsets of Renderer Phase 8 under the
[cross-phase rule](../design/ROADMAP_POLICY.md#cross-phase-delivery) and does
not complete it. No stage demonstrated a lighting gap, so light selection stays
in Renderer Phase 9. Ongoing scope and release targets remain in the
[canonical roadmap](../roadmap/README.md#status-at-a-glance).

## Shipped capabilities

- **MaterialX Standard Surface.** The delegate requests MaterialX networks and
  translates a subset of `ND_standard_surface_surfaceshader` into the material
  IR. A material's MaterialX surface wins over its `UsdPreviewSurface`, as in
  Storm, and inputs the IR cannot represent warn. OpenChessSet and
  StandardShaderBall now show their authored materials
  ([evidence](../reports/2026-10-29-materialx-standard-surface.md)).
- **Dielectric transmission.** Both integrators refract through smooth and
  rough interfaces of solids and thin walls under a
  [transport contract](../design/TRANSMISSION.md), checked against analytic
  oracles and a white furnace. OpenChessSet's pawn tops and the shader ball's
  glass render as tinted glass
  ([evidence](../reports/2026-10-29-dielectric-transmission.md)).
- **Texture transforms, per-face materials and colour primvars.**
  `UsdTransform2d` chains, `GeomSubset` material bindings, colour primvars on the
  base colour, and constant `displayOpacity` on unbound meshes all translate
  ([evidence](../reports/2026-10-31-texture-transforms-subsets-primvars.md)).
- **Skinned meshes.** The delegate runs the ext-computations that output a
  mesh's points and normals on the CPU during sync, so `UsdSkel` meshes render
  at their posed points instead of being rejected or drawn at rest. A committed
  fixture matches OpenUSD's skinning through both of UsdImaging's front ends
  ([evidence](../reports/2026-10-31-skinned-meshes.md)).
- **Fallback environment image.** Through the Hydra plugin, a scene with no dome
  and no scene light is lit by OpenUSD's Stinson Beach image instead of a white
  environment. Camera samples that miss show any environment image, following
  usdview's Dome Light Textures Visible
  ([evidence](../reports/2026-10-31-fallback-environment.md)).

Five committed fixtures cover these features, each with a C++ mirror and
4096-spp references, and Hydra's images equal the mirror's. Detailed changes are
in the [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md#080---2026-10-31); the
[release gate](../reports/2026-10-31-v0.8.0-release-gate.md) records
verification.

## Compatibility and delivery

The `v0.8.0` tag publishes the Windows x86_64 CY2026 lookdev/Hydra package
`lotus-0.8.0-cy2026-windows-x86_64-py313-lookdev--hydra.tar.zst`, its manifest
and SPDX SBOM, `hydra-lotus-0.8.0-src.tar.gz` and `SHA256SUMS` through the
[release workflow](../guides/RELEASING.md). The binary package requires
matching OpenUSD 26.08/Python 3.13 and Vulkan runtimes. It contains the Hydra
plugin and headless runner, not the viewport, which builds from source with the
[building guide](../guides/BUILDING.md#the-standalone-viewport). The package
gains the five fixtures' references and, in `lib/usd/hdLotus/assets`, the
fallback image with OpenUSD's licence and notice.

Stages render differently through the Hydra plugin where this release translates
what was previously ignored, and where the fallback environment replaces white
or a clear background. Directly created delegates, `lotus-viewport` among them,
keep the white environment.

`Material` adds `transmission`, `transmission_color`, `thin_walled` and
`base_color_primvar`; a transmissive specular-workflow material throws.
`TextureInput` adds `st_transform`, `MeshGeometry` adds `colors`,
`PathTracingSettings` adds `environment_background`, off by default, and the
headless report adds `renderer.material.transmission`. A mesh with bound
`GeomSubset`s becomes several scene meshes keyed `<path>.lotus:subset:<name>`.

Three committed references changed on purpose, one card each, on the cards whose
authored feature now translates: `material-textures-v1`, `material-emission-v1`
and `material-alpha-v1`. Every other committed reference is byte-identical
([evidence](../reports/2026-10-31-texture-transforms-subsets-primvars.md)).

## Known limitations at release

- MaterialX support is the Standard Surface subset. Subsurface, coat, OpenPBR,
  `ND_tiledimage` tiling and MaterialX colour readers warn or are not read.
- Transmission has no volume absorption or scattering, dispersion, nested
  dielectrics or textured weight. Shadow rays treat glass as opaque, so caustics
  come only from paths that hit a light
  ([deferred](../reports/2026-10-29-dielectric-transmission.md#deferred)).
- Non-constant `displayOpacity`, faces an invisible `GeomSubset` hides and colour
  primvars on inputs other than the base colour are not supported
  ([deferred](../reports/2026-10-31-texture-transforms-subsets-primvars.md#deferred)).
- Skinning runs on the CPU during sync, and its cost on large meshes and skinned
  meshes under instancers are unmeasured. Lotus renders only mesh rprims, so
  curves and points stay out
  ([not covered](../reports/2026-10-31-skinned-meshes.md#not-covered)).
- The fallback image's pole stays on world +Y, so a Z-up stage's horizon stands
  on end ([limitation](../reports/2026-10-31-fallback-environment.md#limitation)).
- `usdrecord --renderer Storm` creates the Lotus renderer when `hdLotus` is on
  the plugin path; the cause is unexplained
  ([active roadmap](../roadmap/current.md)).
- The viewport intermittently loses the Vulkan device at its second frame on the
  measured NVIDIA driver; headless and Hydra offscreen rendering are unaffected
  ([limitation](../reference/SUPPORTED_CONFIGURATIONS.md#driver-limitations)).
- Linux, macOS and AMD/Intel GPUs are unmeasured.

Remaining work is in the [active roadmap](../roadmap/current.md).
