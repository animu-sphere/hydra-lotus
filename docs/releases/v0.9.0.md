# v0.9.0 — Interactive quality release

Released on 2026-11-09 under Apache-2.0. This release completes the
[v0.9.0 Interactive Quality milestone](../roadmap/README.md#v090--interactive-quality):
still-image convergence and moving-view behaviour are measured separately,
each kind of scene change invalidates only the history it affects, and
Interactive, Balanced and Reference are presets of the same core under an
[accepted contract](../design/INTERACTIVE_QUALITY.md). It ships a cross-phase
delivery under the
[cross-phase rule](../design/ROADMAP_POLICY.md#cross-phase-delivery) and
completes no renderer phase. Reference stays the default, so a host that
selects no preset renders as in v0.8.0. Ongoing scope and release targets
remain in the [canonical roadmap](../roadmap/README.md#status-at-a-glance).

## Shipped capabilities

- **Fixed fixtures and a still/moving baseline.** `lotus-headless
  --benchmark-svgf` runs the contract's five workloads: the SVGF room still
  and moving, a shadow swept over a still floor, a camera panning over a
  roughness-0.1 metal floor and a waving sheet of fixed topology. Each
  reports convergence, frame-delta MSE, its lag region's MSE and resets by
  reason, beside the Reference preset's progressive image
  ([evidence](../reports/2026-11-08-interactive-baseline.md)).
- **Interactive and Reference presets.** `lotus:interactive` through Hydra,
  `--preset` and a Quality list in the viewport. Interactive is temporal
  radiance with a 32-sample history cap and five SVGF iterations; Reference
  is the progressive image. Both hosts map a preset through
  `Lotus::ApplyQualityPreset`, which never changes the estimator, and
  Reference is byte-identical to the committed references
  ([evidence](../reports/2026-11-08-interactive-presets.md)).
- **The invalidation table.** An emitter's rigid motion resets the history;
  a non-emitter's deformation with unchanged topology rejects only that
  mesh's pixels; the sample target and history cap no longer reset it;
  Hydra cuts it when the render pass changes camera prim. On the deforming
  sheet the temporal mean's error falls sevenfold, 0.363 to 0.051, with no
  resets after the cut
  ([evidence](../reports/2026-11-08-interactive-presets.md#effect-on-the-fixed-fixtures)).
- **Balanced settling.** `lotus:balanced`, `--preset balanced` and *Settle
  when still*. Balanced is Interactive while anything changes. Once the view
  is still, the last changing frame's filtered colour crossfades into a
  progressive accumulation of still samples, which at the sample target
  equals the Reference image bit for bit under both integrators, misses
  included. No transition frame steps above the moving view's frame-delta
  MSE, and Hydra reports convergence and traces nothing more
  ([evidence](../reports/2026-11-08-balanced-settling.md)).
- **Measured ghosting candidates.** A neighbourhood clamp, anti-lag and
  roughness-aware reflection weighting are available as the experimental
  `TemporalFrameSettings::history_rectification`. Each lowered the lag
  metric and raised another of the contract's rows, so none is adopted and
  no preset selects one
  ([measured negative](../reports/2026-11-09-ghosting-candidates.md#decision)).

Detailed changes are in the
[changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md#090---2026-11-09); the
[release gate](../reports/2026-11-09-v0.9.0-release-gate.md) records
verification.

## Compatibility and delivery

The `v0.9.0` tag publishes the Windows x86_64 CY2026 lookdev/Hydra package
`lotus-0.9.0-cy2026-windows-x86_64-py313-lookdev--hydra.tar.zst`, its manifest
and SPDX SBOM, `hydra-lotus-0.9.0-src.tar.gz` and `SHA256SUMS` through the
[release workflow](../guides/RELEASING.md). The binary package requires
matching OpenUSD 26.08/Python 3.13 and Vulkan runtimes. It contains the Hydra
plugin and headless runner, not the viewport, which builds from source with the
[building guide](../guides/BUILDING.md#the-standalone-viewport).

Hosts that turn on temporal radiance themselves see the invalidation table's
changes: an emitter's rigid motion now resets the whole history, a
non-emitter's same-topology deformation keeps the history of every other
pixel, and changing `convergedSamplesPerPixel` or `lotus:historySamples` no
longer resets it. The first temporal frame after progressive frames reports
`RenderSettingsChanged` instead of `ExplicitReset`.

`include/lotus/temporal.hpp` adds `QualityPreset`, `ApplyQualityPreset`,
`kInteractiveHistorySamples`, `SettleTemporalRadiance`,
`TemporalHistoryRectification` and `TemporalFrameSettings::settle` and
`history_rectification`; `GpuFrameEvidence` adds `settling`,
`settled_samples` and `settle_gpu_ms`. Hydra adds the `lotus:interactive`
and `lotus:balanced` render settings, both false by default. The
`--benchmark-svgf` report schema is `lotus-svgf-benchmark-v3`. Every
committed reference image is unchanged.

## Known limitations at release

- No ghosting fix is adopted. Interactive and moving Balanced lag where the
  [baseline](../reports/2026-11-08-interactive-baseline.md) measured it, in
  swept shadows and over low-roughness metal; adopting a candidate needs a
  contract change, not a new fixture
  ([decision](../reports/2026-11-09-ghosting-candidates.md#decision)).
- The emitter rule is by material, not by measured contribution, so an
  emitter hidden behind everything still resets the history when it moves.
  Switching to a camera prim with an identical pose still cuts
  ([limits](../reports/2026-11-08-interactive-presets.md#limits)).
- Balanced never settles with reservoir reuse. A view held twice shows the
  same noise twice, as Reference does, and leaving a settled view returns to
  the last changing frame's history
  ([limits](../reports/2026-11-08-balanced-settling.md#limits)).
- The milestone's timings were taken at dynamic clocks and are not cost
  evidence.
- `usdrecord --renderer Storm` creates the Lotus renderer when `hdLotus` is on
  the plugin path; the cause is unexplained
  ([active roadmap](../roadmap/current.md)).
- The viewport intermittently loses the Vulkan device at its second frame on the
  measured NVIDIA driver; headless and Hydra offscreen rendering are unaffected
  ([limitation](../reference/SUPPORTED_CONFIGURATIONS.md#driver-limitations)).
- Linux, macOS and AMD/Intel GPUs are unmeasured.

Remaining work is in the [active roadmap](../roadmap/current.md).
