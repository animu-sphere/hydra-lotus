# v0.6.0 — Interactive rendering release

Released on 2026-10-18 under Apache-2.0. This release completes the
[v0.6.0 plan](../roadmap/README.md#v060--interactive-rendering): Lotus opens
a scene on its own, path-traces it progressively in the standalone viewport,
and lets a developer observe and switch its temporal, ReSTIR and denoising
state while it runs. Renderer Phase 6 adds the SVGF-class denoiser. The
ReSTIR DI closure ([v0.5.1](v0.5.1.md)) and the viewport foundation
([v0.5.2](v0.5.2.md)) shipped ahead of it. Rendering defaults are unchanged:
the denoiser is off unless a host asks for it. Ongoing scope and release
targets remain in the
[canonical roadmap](../roadmap/README.md#status-at-a-glance).

## Shipped capabilities

- **Moments and variance.** The temporal radiance accumulation keeps the
  first and second moments of each batch's illumination luminance, the
  colour demodulated by the surface albedo, and a variance pass estimates
  each pixel's variance: temporally from four batches of history, with
  SVGF's edge-aware 7x7 spatial estimate before. Raw radiance, history
  length, moments, variance and rejection reasons are products with their
  GPU times, checked at every pixel against independent batches and a CPU
  oracle under both integrators
  ([evidence](../reports/2026-10-18-svgf-moments-variance.md)).
- **Edge-aware filtering.** SVGF's à-trous filter runs over the
  accumulated radiance, guided by depth, shading normals, stable identity
  and illumination variance, and the composite remodulates by the albedo;
  the history stays unfiltered. It reproduces a CPU oracle at every pixel.
  Against a high-spp reference at one sample per frame it cuts the first
  frame's error by four, halves the error under motion and reduces flicker
  and just-disoccluded error by about three; on a view held still, the
  temporal mean overtakes it within about fourteen frames. Both integrators
  give identical outputs, reproduced bit for bit
  ([evidence](../reports/2026-10-18-svgf-filter.md)).
- **Denoising in the viewport.** `--denoise`, F2 and the developer UI switch
  the filter and its iteration count at run time without resetting the
  history; raw, accumulated, history-length and variance views come from the
  GPU, and a split view compares any of them with the filtered image. The
  timing HUD and `--benchmark` time the variance and filter passes and show
  the filter's memory. Compact per-pixel guides cut five iterations at
  1280x720 from 11.6 to 2.4 ms
  ([evidence](../reports/2026-10-18-svgf-viewport.md)).
- **Hydra.** `lotus:filterIterations` filters `lotus:temporal`'s colour
  ([reference](../reference/TEMPORAL.md#hydra-controls-and-debug-aovs)).
- **Test tiers.** Every CTest carries one `fast`, `gpu` or `research` tier
  label, checked by `lotus-test-tiers`; Renderer Phase 5's campaign variants
  and the SVGF quality campaign run in the `research` intent, and
  documentation-only pull requests skip the CI builds
  ([policy](../design/TEST_POLICY.md),
  [evidence](../reports/2026-10-18-test-tiers.md)).

With these, Renderer Phase 6 meets its
[exit criteria](../reports/2026-10-18-svgf-viewport.md#renderer-phase-6-exit).
Detailed changes, including the API additions behind them, are in the
[changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md#060---2026-10-18); the
[release gate](../reports/2026-10-18-v0.6.0-release-gate.md) records
verification.

## Compatibility and delivery

The `v0.6.0` tag publishes the Windows x86_64 CY2026 lookdev/Hydra package
`lotus-0.6.0-cy2026-windows-x86_64-py313-lookdev--hydra.tar.zst`, its manifest
and SPDX SBOM, `hydra-lotus-0.6.0-src.tar.gz` and `SHA256SUMS` through the
[release workflow](../guides/RELEASING.md). The binary package requires
matching OpenUSD 26.08/Python 3.13 and Vulkan runtimes. It contains the Hydra
plugin and headless runner, not the viewport, which builds from source with
the [building guide](../guides/BUILDING.md#the-standalone-viewport).

Measured configuration: Windows 11 x86_64, MSVC 14.51, OpenStrata 0.23.14,
NVIDIA RTX A5000 (driver 597.16) and OpenUSD 26.08/Python 3.13 for Hydra.

`TemporalRadiancePixel`'s reserved words became `moments` and `variance`, and
`TemporalPixel`'s became `albedo` and `shading_normal`; both records keep
their sizes. Temporal radiance memory is 160 bytes per pixel (was 144), and
a filtering frame adds 48 bytes per pixel. The temporal timestamp pool has
ten queries. `TemporalProducts` adds `batch`, `variance_gpu_ms`,
`filter_iterations`, `filter_gpu_ms` and `filter_bytes`. `DebugView` adds
four values and `PresentView` a `split`. Eighteen ReSTIR campaign CTests
moved to the `research` tier, which a default build no longer registers.

## Known limitations at release

- The viewport intermittently loses the Vulkan device at its second frame on
  the measured NVIDIA driver; headless and Hydra offscreen rendering are
  unaffected
  ([limitation](../reference/SUPPORTED_CONFIGURATIONS.md#driver-limitations),
  [report](../reports/2026-10-18-viewport-device-loss.md)).
- The filter's quality is measured in one workload at one resolution and one
  sample per frame. Demodulation treats glossy reflection and emission as
  diffuse illumination, silhouette pixels mix two surfaces' light, and there
  is no firefly suppression or feature-adaptive filtering yet
  ([limits](../reports/2026-10-18-svgf-filter.md#limits)).
- The viewport has no moments view, and its split compares two products of
  one frame, not the filter's temporal stability.
- Reuse modes hold 1,280 bytes per pixel of reservoirs, half of it host
  mirrors a presented frame does not read
  ([memory](../reports/2026-10-18-viewport-timing.md#memory)).
- The viewport presents linear radiance clamped to sRGB without exposure or
  tone mapping.
- Linux, macOS and AMD/Intel GPUs are unmeasured.

Remaining work is in the [active roadmap](../roadmap/current.md).
