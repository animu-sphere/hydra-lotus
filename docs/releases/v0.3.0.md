# v0.3.0 — Direct lighting release

Released on 2026-10-12 under Apache-2.0. This release adds Renderer Phase 3:
next event estimation with multiple importance sampling towards emissive
geometry, UsdLux analytic lights and textured dome lights, in both
integrators, headless, in the benchmark and through Hydra. Light sampling is
the default; brute-force BSDF sampling stays selectable and still
reproduces the committed reference bit for bit.

## Shipped capabilities

- `PathTracingSettings::light_sampling` selects `Mis` (the default) or
  `Bsdf`. Under `Mis`, each scattering event chooses a light from one
  alias-method table in proportion to its estimated power, traces a shadow
  ray to it, with partial coverage as transmittance, and weighs light
  samples and the emission BSDF-sampled directions find by the power
  heuristic. Both integrators run it in the same random-number order.
- Emissive triangles of any material are sampled by area, in proportion to
  area times emission.
- `Lotus::Light` holds UsdLux sphere, rect, disk and distant lights in the
  core scene; next event estimation samples a sphere's cone, a rect or disk
  by area and a distant light's cone, and a ray adds the radiance of the
  lights it crosses. The Hydra adapter translates `sphereLight`,
  `rectLight`, `diskLight` and `distantLight` sprims.
- `Lotus::EnvironmentMap` holds a latitude-longitude environment image in
  the OpenEXR layout, sampled in proportion to its radiance. Textured
  `domeLight` sprims become it, oriented by their transform after
  `domeOffset`.
- The `lotus:lightSampling` Hydra render setting, listed by usdview as the
  checked *Light sampling (NEE + MIS)* item in its Hydra Settings menu,
  selects the estimator.
- OpenUSD's Stinson Beach image, its licence and a manifest are committed
  as validation assets, installed beside `lotus-headless` and required in
  packages.
- The benchmark selects the estimator with `--benchmark-light-sampling`
  (default `bsdf`, so that earlier measurements stay comparable), runs two
  lit workloads beside the four fixed ones, and reports per-sample
  variance and a measure proportional to the time to equal quality.

## Correctness and measurement

Light sampling is checked against the committed reference in the Cornell
box, against BSDF sampling's estimate in open scenes, and against
independent double-precision irradiance oracles for each analytic light
and for the Stinson Beach image, under both estimators and integrators,
by the [reference tolerance](../reference/SCENE.md#reference-images). The
committed reference images were not regenerated.

On the measured device light sampling shortens the time to equal quality
against BSDF sampling 2.6× in the Cornell box under the reference
integrator and 3.0× under the wavefront integrator, and by three orders of
magnitude or more under analytic lights and the Stinson Beach image.
Scenes lit by a constant environment alone are unchanged: that
environment stays with BSDF sampling, which samples it better.

## Compatibility and delivery

The `v0.3.0` tag publishes, through GitHub Actions, the Windows x86_64
CY2026 lookdev/Hydra package
`lotus-0.3.0-cy2026-windows-x86_64-py313-lookdev--hydra.tar.zst` with its
manifest and SBOM, the source archive `hydra-lotus-0.3.0-src.tar.gz` and
`SHA256SUMS`. The package needs a matching OpenUSD 26.08/Python 3.13 runtime
and Vulkan; it does not bundle them. Other configurations build from source
with the [building guide](../guides/BUILDING.md).

Measured configuration: Windows 11 x86_64, MSVC 14.51, OpenStrata 0.23.14,
NVIDIA RTX A5000 and OpenUSD 26.08/Python 3.13 for Hydra. The GPU
requirements are unchanged and listed in the
[configuration reference](../reference/SUPPORTED_CONFIGURATIONS.md#requirements).

Public API changes since v0.2.0 are additions: `LightSampling`,
`PathTracingSettings::light_sampling`, `LightShape`, `Light`,
`LotusScene::lights`, `RenderWorld::SetLight` and `RemoveLight`,
`EnvironmentMap`, `EnvironmentFrame`, `LotusScene::environment_map`,
`RenderWorld::SetEnvironmentMap`, `SceneEnvironmentMap`,
`SceneUpdate::lights_changed`, `lights` and `environment_map`, and the
`GpuSceneStats` light-table counters. Behaviour changes for existing
callers:

- `light_sampling` defaults to `Mis`, so default images converge faster to
  the same mean but are no longer BSDF sampling's samples; set `Bsdf` to
  reproduce v0.2.0's images.
- Through Hydra, `lotus:lightSampling` defaults to true; any synced
  analytic light, like any dome, replaces the adapter's white fallback
  environment; and a textured dome lights the scene with its image instead
  of a uniform radiance.

## Known limitations at release

- Every ray tests every analytic light, so a frame's cost grows linearly
  with their number. Acceleration for many lights is assigned to Renderer
  Phase 9 (LOTUS-LIGHT-05).
- Light textures other than the dome's, shaping (cone and IES), shadow
  controls, colour temperature, light linking, `treatAsPoint`, cylinder,
  portal, geometry and mesh lights, and more than one textured dome are not
  supported; the constant environment is not light-sampled.
- The wavefront integrator is 2.1–2.8 times slower per sample than the
  reference integrator at 128×128, so the reference integrator stays the
  default. Path classification is deferred to Renderer Phase 8.
- ReSTIR, temporal reconstruction, denoising and spectral rendering are
  outside this snapshot.
- The standalone viewport presents the bootstrap triangle; the offscreen
  renderer has one frame in flight and blocking CPU readback. Hydra ID
  channels are CPU sentinels, so picking is unavailable.
- Material and texture translation is the minimal Renderer Phase 1.5
  subset. Production MaterialX/OpenPBR and glass/transmission are outside
  this snapshot.
- Linux, macOS and AMD/Intel GPUs have not been measured, and only the
  measured Windows configuration is packaged. Japanese MSVC dependency
  discovery and OpenStrata workflow/runtime-free validation selection
  require the documented workarounds.

The precise constraints and rationales are recorded in the
[configuration limitations](../reference/SUPPORTED_CONFIGURATIONS.md#foundation-bootstrap-limitations),
[scene/material reference](../reference/SCENE.md) and
[AOV contract](../reference/AOVS.md). Future implementation work is tracked
in the [active roadmap](../roadmap/current.md).
