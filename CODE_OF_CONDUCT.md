# Code of Conduct

Hydra Lotus contributors and maintainers should be able to participate
without harassment. This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews,
discussions and project channels, and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Expectations

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique proposals and code, not people.
- Welcome different backgrounds and experience levels. Ask for context,
  explain your reasoning and accept corrections without personal attacks.
- Respect others' time, boundaries and privacy.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against or deliberately intimidate
  others, or share someone's private information without consent.
- Do not engage in unwanted sexual attention, repeated unwanted contact or
  disruptive behavior after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

For abusive content hosted on GitHub, use GitHub's
[report-content process](https://github.com/contact/report-content).
If a maintainer publishes a private contact method on their GitHub profile,
you may also use it for a project conduct concern. If no private channel is
available, open an issue requesting one without naming people or describing
the incident. Do not post sensitive details publicly. Suspected
vulnerabilities follow the separate process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers consider the context, protect reporters' privacy as far as
practical and avoid public discussion of sensitive reports. A maintainer
involved in a report should defer its review to another maintainer where
possible. Depending on the behavior, maintainers may request a change in
conduct, issue a warning, remove content, close or lock a thread, or restrict
participation. Serious or repeated violations may lead to a permanent
restriction.

You may request reconsideration privately through an available maintainer
contact method. Include relevant context and respect the privacy of everyone
involved.
